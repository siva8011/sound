About

AstroDither is an audio reactive TSL & WebGPU experiment built with ThreeJS. This started after seeing an online GIF — a decision to recreate it, exploring new tech like TSL with WebGPU.

Nothing fancy, just goooood old R&D.

Some Random Tech

Despite what you think, a lot is happening here. There is a custom dither on the model only, needed to mix with a generic scene and a custom fluid built from scratch in a single pass for performance. On top of that a dither + displacement in postprocessing and selective bloom without masks — just fancy tricks. Oh wait, did you see the aberration?
